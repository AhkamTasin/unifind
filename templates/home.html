How it works

A simple, secure process from report to recovery.

1. Report

Report a lost or found item with photos and details.

2. Submit to Desk

Found items are physically handed to the Lost & Found Desk.

3. Verify & Track

The desk verifies the item and assigns a unique tracking ID.

4. Claim & Recover

Owners submit claims; approved claims collect their items.
