How It Works

Every found item is physically verified at the Campus Lost & Found Desk before it is published.

I lost an item
Recovering your belongings
  1. Report the lost item with details and a photo.
  2. Search found items at the desk — you can filter by category, location and date.
  3. Submit a claim on a verified found item that matches, describing how you can prove ownership.
  4. The Desk Officer reviews your claim and approves or rejects it.
  5. Collect your item from the desk with your ID and supporting proof.
I found an item
Helping someone recover it
  1. Report the found item online with a photo and where you found it.
  2. Physically submit the item to the Campus Lost & Found Desk (Student Services Building, Room 102).
  3. The desk verifies the item and assigns a unique tracking ID.
  4. Owners claim the item; the desk verifies each claim.
  5. The item is returned to its rightful owner and the case is resolved.
Why physical verification?

Digital reporting alone makes it easy for fake or unverified posts to appear. By requiring every found item to be physically handed to the Lost & Found Desk before publishing, and by having the Desk Officer review every ownership claim, the system keeps records reliable and reduces fraudulent claims.

Tracking IDs

Every verified found item receives a unique tracking ID such as LF-2026-4K7QX9. You can use this ID to refer to an item when talking to the desk or when submitting a claim.
