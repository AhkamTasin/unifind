About the System

UniFind — Campus Lost & Found Management System · CSE 338 Web Technologies Sessional

UniFind (the Campus Lost & Found Management System) is a web-based application designed to manage lost and found items within a university campus. In many universities, lost and found activities are managed through informal methods such as social media, notice boards, or verbal communication — making the process inefficient, unorganized, and difficult to manage.

This system provides a centralized platform where students, teachers, and staff can report lost or found items, search available records, and submit ownership claims. Every found item must be physically submitted to the Campus Lost & Found Desk before its report is approved. The admin verifies the submitted item, assigns a unique tracking ID, reviews ownership claims, and ensures recovered items are returned to their rightful owners.

Objectives

Centralized reporting, desk verification, unique tracking IDs, secure claim handling and faster recovery.

Target Users

Students, teachers and university staff report items; the Lost & Found Desk Officer verifies and manages cases.

Technology

Django (Python) backend with MySQL database, Bootstrap 5, HTML5, CSS3 and JavaScript frontend.
